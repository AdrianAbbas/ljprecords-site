About

LJP Records Inc.

An independent record label and music company based in Mississauga, Ontario.

Who we are

LJP Records Inc. works closely with artists, producers and composers to make records that sound like the people who made them. Our work covers the full life of a song: writing and composing, production, artist development, and release. Beyond records, we compose original music for media, including film, games and other visual projects.

Our approach

We stay independent on purpose. Our roster is small, the collaboration is hands-on, and every release gets the attention it deserves. We'd rather build an artist's catalogue over years than chase a single moment.

Elsewhere Audio

Elsewhere Audio, a division of LJP Records Inc., is our audio software line: virtual instruments and effects for musicians, producers and composers.
