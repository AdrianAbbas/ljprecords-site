About

Music made with intent.

LJP Records Inc. is an independent record label and music company based in Mississauga, Ontario. We work closely with artists, producers and composers to make records that sound like the people who made them.

Our work covers the full life of a song: writing and composing, production, artist development, and release. Beyond records, we compose original music for media, including film, games and other visual projects.

We stay independent on purpose. Our roster is small, the collaboration is hands-on, and every release gets the attention it deserves.

Jasy

R&B / Soul · Mississauga, Ontario

Jasy is a singer, songwriter and composer from Mississauga, Ontario, known for soulful vocals, introspective lyrics and a bilingual touch. Her early singles include “CRIMINAL” (2021), sung in French and English, and “BELLADONNA.” Her first release with LJP Records, “no idea, honestly,” arrived in October 2025, followed by the 2026 singles “Charge it up” and “Away.”

Hollowsound

Alternative rock · Toronto, Ontario

Hollowsound is a Toronto-based alternative rock band blending grunge, shoegaze and hard rock. Their songs deal with isolation and nostalgia, built on loud guitars and atmospheric textures. The band is Adrian Abbas (vocals, guitar), Jonathan Connors (bass), Matthew De Matas-Villagomez (guitar) and Daniel Markusson (drums). Their debut single, “Melancholia,” is out now.
