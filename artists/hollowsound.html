Artist

Hollowsound

Alternative rock · Toronto, Ontario

Hollowsound is a Toronto-based alternative rock band blending grunge, shoegaze and hard rock. Their songs deal with isolation and nostalgia, built on loud guitars and atmospheric textures. The band is Adrian Abbas (vocals, guitar), Jonathan Connors (bass), Matthew De Matas-Villagomez (guitar) and Daniel Markusson (drums). Their debut single, “Melancholia,” is out now.
