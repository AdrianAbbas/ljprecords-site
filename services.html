Services

What we do

We work with artists, producers and composers at every stage, from first idea to finished release.

Music production

Production, arrangement and recording that serve the song and the artist's identity. We build records from early demos through to final mixes.

Composing

Original songs written for and with artists, plus score and original music for media: film, games and other visual projects.

Collaboration & co-writing

Writing and production sessions with artists, songwriters and producers to shape ideas into finished songs.

Artist development

Long-term, hands-on support for an artist's sound, catalogue and direction.

Releases & distribution

Planning and delivering releases to streaming platforms and stores worldwide.
